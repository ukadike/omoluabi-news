Sovereignty over observation

Omoluabi News is not merely a news website. It is an editorial reasoning platform built on one premise: the person who noticed something controls how it's held, when it moves, what form it takes, and who benefits.

The infrastructure makes premature extraction structurally impossible by holding the interval open for consent, context, and care — on terms set by the observer, not the system.

How a story is reasoned

Every story carries twelve editorial reasoning layers, evidence ranked by a source ladder, and a timeline integrity check. None of it is hidden — expand a story's reasoning section to see the same questions an editor asked before publishing.

Omoluabi News is part of the Omoluabi research initiative. See the Omoluabi and Small Systems Lab repositories for governance and architecture.
